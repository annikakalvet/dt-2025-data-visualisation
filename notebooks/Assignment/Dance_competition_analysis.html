plt.show
Out[18]:
<function matplotlib.pyplot.show(close=None, block=None)>

From this chart, we can conclude that there is no obvious correlation between age and points. Younger dancers seem to be more in the bottom left corner, meaning they get lower points. And older dancers' points are more in the top right corner or in the middle top, meaning they get more points. But we can see many outliers, like 11-year-old who got 20p(max), or 17-year-old who got 7p. 16-20-year-olds seem to get almost the same points, meaning they could be at a very similar level.
